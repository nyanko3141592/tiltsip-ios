# App Store Review Notes

Water Sim is an iPhone-only, portrait-only entertainment app. It has no login,
network-backed features, advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases.

## Review flow

1. Complete the three-page first-launch onboarding.
2. Hold the device upright in portrait orientation.
3. Tilt the device left or right. The liquid surface remains world-horizontal.
4. At approximately 40 degrees, liquid begins to spill from the visible top rim.
5. Tap the liquid to switch between beer and cola.
6. Tap the drink label at the top of the safe area to open information and refill.

Front-to-back pitch is intentionally ignored because the app presents a
side-view cross-section of the phone as a glass.

Core Motion values are processed temporarily on device and are never retained
or transmitted. The app declares no collected data and does not track users.
