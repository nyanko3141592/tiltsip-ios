Last updated: August 6, 2026

TiltSip does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data.

The app reads the iPhone's current motion sensor values to render the liquid in
response to left and right tilt. Those values are processed temporarily on the
device and are not saved or sent to the developer or any third party.

TiltSip contains no advertising, analytics, account system, or third-party
tracking SDK. The app does not track users across apps or websites.

If these practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will
be updated before the relevant version is released.

For privacy questions, use the contact method on the [support page](../support/).

## 日本語

TiltSipは、個人データを収集、保存、送信、共有しません。

液体を左右の傾きに反応させるため、iPhoneの現在のモーションセンサー値を端末内で一時的に処理します。この値は保存されず、開発者や第三者へ送信されません。

広告、アクセス解析、アカウント機能、第三者トラッキングSDKは含まれていません。
